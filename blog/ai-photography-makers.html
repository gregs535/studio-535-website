Turn Phone Photos Into Studio-Grade Product Shots With Free AI

Published by Studio 535 · Precision Crafted. Technology Engineered.

Your product is beautiful in person. In the photo? Flat, dim, lost against a cluttered counter. Buyers shop with their eyes first — on Etsy, Instagram, Pinterest — and a weak photo quietly kills sales no matter how good the making is.

You do not need a $2,000 camera or a lightbox. You need a phone, a window, and a free AI edit pipeline. Here is ours.

Step 1: Shoot With $0 Equipment

  • Set up next to a window with indirect light. No direct sun (it blows out highlights).
  • Put the product on a clean surface — plain wood, white poster board, or a neutral fabric.
  • Shoot 6 angles: front, 3/4, top-down, detail/macro, in-use, and lifestyle.
  • Lock exposure and tap to focus on the product, not the background.

Step 2: The Edit Pipeline

Run every hero shot through four free steps:

Clean

Remove dust, scratches, and stray reflections with a free AI photo editor (most offer a free tier).

Upscale

Push to at least 2000px on the long edge so Etsy doesn't compress it into mush. Free upscalers exist for 2x-4x.

Background

Drop the background to pure white (Etsy's preference) or a consistent brand color. Free background-removal tools handle this in one click.

Relight

If the window light was uneven, use a free relight tool to even out shadows and add a soft highlight. Subtle wins; don't overdo it.

Step 3: Export Per Platform

  • Etsy: 2000px long edge, white background, under 2MB, JPEG quality 85.
  • Instagram: 1080x1080 square or 1080x1350 portrait, sRGB.
  • Pinterest: 1000x1500 vertical, the highest-converting ratio for product pins.
